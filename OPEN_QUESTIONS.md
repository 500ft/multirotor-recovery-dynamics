# Open Questions

Track unresolved design decisions here. Close each item with evidence rather than removing it silently.

The platform is now the Bitcraze Crazyflie (owner decision, 2026-10-04). Its
questions are OQ-018 to OQ-021. The V995 is retired: OQ-011 is closed, and
OQ-012 and OQ-017 (load-cell choice and revision) are deferred until a
Crazyflie is bought for bench work. OQ-001, OQ-002, OQ-005 and OQ-007 to OQ-009 belong to the
historical designed vehicle, which is not being built; they stay here as
record. The plan and finish line are in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).
