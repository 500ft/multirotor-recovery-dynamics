3. FINDINGS THAT DETERMINE THE ORDER
===================================

These are source-inspection findings, not claims of new experimentally reproduced
failures. Where a behavior needs a runtime counterexample, the work order requires
one before fixing it.

F01 — A2 changes the controller AND the simulated plant.
  At the candidate head, survivable_set._realloc_trial sets yaw drag only for a2
  and simultaneously enables spin_aware. A-vs-A2 therefore cannot isolate the
  feedforward contribution. It may be reported as a combined-model comparison,
  but not as a controlled estimate of the controller change. Source: candidate
  Analysis/survivable_set.py and docs/specs/survivable-set/design-a2.md.

F02 — A flight-failure study reuses a release/startup transient.
  simulate() sets all thrust and control torque to zero until detection latency
  plus motor-start latency. Healthy motors are also off during this interval.
  That is a definite modeled sequence, but it is not automatically the sequence
  of an already flying quad suffering one motor failure. Quantify its effect;
  do not label the assumption conservative without qualification. Source:
  Analysis/sim_release_recovery.py, passive_time and motors_on branches.

F03 — Quick runs write to the same result paths as full runs.
  In the candidate main(), --quick changes trial counts and the note, but data
  still goes to Data/survivable_set_results{,_a2}.json. This can replace a full
  result with a smoke-sized result. Separate these paths before further runs.

F04 — Impact quantities are taken from the sample before the crossing.
  simulate() logs state, advances one step, then detects ground contact;
  _realloc_trial reads the last logged velocity/tilt. That sample precedes the
  actual crossing. Bound the error and introduce an explicit touchdown event.

F05 — The convergence evidence is narrower than the claims it could support.
  Candidate run_convergence checks 12 trials, mechanism=True, a2, two classes,
  two primary cells; its summary checks speed deltas and landed agreement.
  It does not summarize criterion flips, tilt deltas, range violations or all
  relevant failure classes. Also, noise is drawn every integration step: halving
  dt changes the stochastic process even when the initial seed is reused.

F06 — The allocator's advertised behavior is internally inconsistent.
  Its module text describes direction-preserving trim allocation, but apply()
  implements a two-pass weighted solve and clipping. Compare the implementation
  to a small independent bounded least-squares oracle before claiming optimality
  or replacing the controller. The numerical priority currently weights N and
  N*m residuals; document units before interpreting TORQUE_PRIORITY=10.
  max_collective() also describes a balanced trim ceiling for two_adjacent,
  where the module itself says exact positive-thrust roll trim is impossible.

F07 — The proposed physical falsification rule is not a calibrated hypothesis test.
  The candidate drop document calls at least one hard failure in ten drops a
  falsification of a prediction with lower bound about 0.905. If the true success
  probability were 0.05**(1/30) = 0.904966, the probability of at least one failure
  in ten independent trials would be 1 - p**10 = 0.631597. An operational safety
  stop may use a single failure; that is different from statistical falsification.
  Also, a Monte Carlo confidence bound under assumed inputs is not a calibrated
  interval for real-world performance. See W06 and primary source S2.

F08 — Candidate Study C assumes facility and injection capabilities not established
       by the inspected repository.
  OQ-006 still asks what heights the actual enclosure supports. A simulation's
  3 m height is not proof of a netted 3 m usable test volume. The state-machine
  specification is not evidence of implemented motor-failure injection firmware.
  Resolve these as prerequisites; do not turn the prediction into an operating plan.

F09 — A ranking exists even when all available actions are poor.
  policy_map() picks a best action in every populated cell. That is useful for
  ranking but does not imply a supported survivable action. kill_criterion()
  also returns its negative verdict for an empty/incomplete comparison set.
  Missing evidence must not be reported as a completed mechanism rejection.

F10 — The current domain is a restricted slice, not a full survivable set.
  Initial tilt uses one axis, tumble uses a fixed diagonal axis by default, and
  each named failure class uses fixed motor indices. Touchdown success checks
  vertical speed and tilt while excluding lateral speed and angular impact.
  Describe this as a conditional landing-proxy map. Do not silently replace its
  criteria after seeing results; register a new diagnostic/validation extension.

8. W05 — MATCHED CONTROLLER/PLANT DIAGNOSTIC
==========================================
Priority: P1; the main new analytical development. Budget: approximately 5 h
of active work, plus bounded measured compute time. Depends on W02–W04.

Proposed new study ID: DR-SS-DIAG-01, provided the ID is unused at execution.
This is a new exploratory diagnostic. It does not amend the frozen A/A2 results
or become a preregistered confirmatory study merely because a plan exists.

Existing files to extend minimally:
  Analysis/survivable_set.py
  Analysis/run_survivable_set.py
  Analysis/tests/test_survivable_set.py
Proposed new protocol:
  docs/specs/survivable-set/diagnostic-01.md
Output:
  a separate run directory chosen through W02, never the old result filenames.

Experimental factors:
  Controller: gyroscopic feedforward OFF / ON.
  Physical yaw-drag coefficient: 0, 6e-6, 1.2e-5 N*m*s^2.
  The drag values are an illustrative zero/nominal/double sensitivity bracket
  around the candidate's estimate. They are not measured limits or a probability
  distribution. Both controllers get exactly the same drag value within a pair.

Hold fixed within each pair:
  allocator revision, descent rule, geometry, mass/inertia, initial state,
  vehicle imperfections, sensor sample stream, integration settings, scenario
  mode, landing criterion, motor failure and trial ID.

Pilot design, proposed and to be recorded before execution:
  Failure classes: one_out and two_opposite.
  Cells, units (m, m/s, rad/s, s):
    (3.0, 0.0, 2.0, 0.11)
    (6.0, 0.0, 2.0, 0.11)
    (3.0, 0.0, 6.0, 0.11)
    (6.0, 0.0, 6.0, 0.11)
  12 paired trials per class/cell/drag setting.
  2 classes * 4 cells * 3 drag values * 12 trials * 2 controllers = 576 runs.
  Use the guarded mass/inertia model for both controllers in this pilot. Score
  both against each identical criterion as a sensitivity report; do not let one
  controller receive an easier criterion. This isolates a controller comparison,
  not the value of the guard. No parachute runs are needed for this question.

Before the 576-run pilot:
  Benchmark eight representative trials, including a full-horizon case. Estimate
  compute cost from those timings and selected worker count. Default to two
  workers on the user's computer until resource availability is established.
  If the projected pilot exceeds a one-hour diagnostic compute allowance, use
  a smaller predeclared diagnostic subset and label it accordingly. Do not alter
  criteria or select only favorable cells to fit the allowance.

Readouts:
  - Paired change in contact vertical/lateral speed, tilt and angular energy.
  - Counts: both proxy-pass; ON-only pass; OFF-only pass; neither pass.
  - Per-axis gyro clipping, actuator saturation and allocation error.
  - Failure reasons and criterion margins for each trial.
  - Results by drag setting and scenario, never just a pooled average.

Interpretation:
  A difference between drag settings with feedforward fixed is a plant-sensitivity
  finding. A difference between OFF and ON with drag fixed is a controller finding
  conditional on that modeled plant. If an apparent gain occurs only at one
  unmeasured drag value, prioritize identifying drag before claiming a robust gain.
  Twelve pairs characterize the pipeline and large patterns; they do not prove
  statistical equivalence or absence of a useful controller effect.

Small actions:
  W05.1 Freeze the diagnostic configuration, factors and pilot counts in the new file.
  W05.2 Separate plant fields from controller flags in the trial call; keep the
        existing variant aliases for historical replay.
  W05.3 Use W02 trial identities to draw the vehicle once and feed the same
        realization to both controllers. Test equality of paired input records.
  W05.4 Add pairwise output summaries and failure categories before the pilot runs.
  W05.5 Benchmark and record the chosen compute scope; run the bounded pilot.
  W05.6 Produce a small controller-vs-drag comparison figure plus a trial ledger.
        Label every figure SIMULATION / EST INPUTS / DIAGNOSTIC.
  W05.7 Write the finding in terms of controller, plant, numerical behavior or
        unresolved uncertainty. Include counterexamples, not just averages.

Acceptance:
  The controller effect can be separated from the assumed drag effect. Every
  surprising trial has a replay command. A gain must survive the matched input

  - “Both actions fail under this model” does not identify the controller as

    construction, with discrete conservatism. Source S2.
