3. PROPOSED MEASUREMENT ARCHITECTURE
==================================

Mechanical load path:
  Drone or test article -> documented cradle/load interface -> selected load cell
  -> fixed bracket -> bench attachment.

Force signal path:
  Load-cell bridge -> NAU7802 -> QT Py -> USB -> computer raw run directory.

Electrical signal path:
  INA260 -> QT Py -> same logger clock/run ID.

Motion signal path:
  LIS3DH on the test article -> QT Py -> same logger clock/run ID.

Event signal path:
  External beam emitter/receiver -> QT Py digital input -> event record.

Initial placement:
  Bench: load cell(s), NAU7802, QT Py, INA260, beam gate and computer.
  Test article: only LIS3DH and its required secured wiring when acceleration of
  the article is the measurement. The load-cell cradle is a restraint, not payload.
  A later onboard package might contain QT Py + LIS3DH + validated power/storage.
  That is conditional on measured mass, CG, supply and recording feasibility.
  USB-tethered data is bench data; a USB cable changes the dynamics of free flight.

Use one load cell at a time for the first calibrated chain. New NAU7802 Rev C
boards expose A and B differential inputs; earlier boards expose only A. Confirm
the actual terminal labels. Two inputs share the conversion chain, so they do not
provide simultaneous independent force samples. Dual-cell operation would need
channel identities, settling, excitation-load and timing checks. It is unnecessary
for the first week's default experiment. [S1, S2]

Recommended initial cell: 1 kg, CONDITIONAL on the installed load budget and
demonstrated resolution. Its nominal mass-equivalent rating corresponds to about
9.81 N using standard gravity; the 5 kg rating corresponds to about 49.0 N. Those
are calculated conversions, not test results or impact ratings. Adafruit advises
capacity of at least twice intended maximum load; use that as an initial selection
screen while assessing preload, dynamic excursions, off-axis load and fixture
strength separately. A tare removes an offset from the data, not physical load.
Do not automatically prefer the 5 kg cell because its number is larger. [S3, S4]

The vendor lists different nominal lengths for the two cells, 80 mm and 75 mm.
This alone rules out assuming one interchangeable mounting drawing. Inspect the
delivered bodies and source drawings before accepting any hole locations. [S3, S4]

Planning-scale calculation using the Owner's approximate 50 g mass:
  Hover-equivalent weight = 0.050 kg * 9.80665 m/s^2 = about 0.49 N.
  This is about 5% of the 1 kg cell range and 1% of the 5 kg cell range.
  Therefore the relevant question is demonstrated low-force resolution, not just
  overload capacity. Maximum thrust is still unknown and is not equal to weight.
  An added 2 g / 5 g / 10 g package would add about 4% / 10% / 20% to a 50 g
  baseline. These are calculated mass fractions, not approved payload allowances.
  The listed 29 g nominal 1 kg cell alone would add about 58%; it belongs in the
  bench apparatus. [S3] Weigh the delivered electronics, mount, wires and power
  hardware before proposing an onboard package; do not assume catalog board mass
  represents the complete installation.


4. ELECTRICAL DESIGN THAT CLAUDE SHOULD MAKE CONCRETE
==================================================

Prepare a connection table with: source board/pin, destination board/pin, voltage,
signal direction, bus identity, wire/connector, source reference and verification.
This is a proposed topology, to be checked against the actual board revisions.

E1. Start with USB power for the QT Py on the bench and 3.3 V sensor logic.
    The QT Py STEMMA connector uses I2C1; its labeled SDA/SCL pads use I2C0.
    In CircuitPython, use board.STEMMA_I2C() for that connector; board.I2C()
    selects the other bus. In Arduino the connector is Wire1. [S5]
    Proposed allocation: NAU7802 via the purchased QT cable on I2C1; INA260 and
    LIS3DH via headers on I2C0. Do not bridge the two buses accidentally.
    Expected addresses: NAU7802 0x2A, INA260 default 0x40, LIS3DH 0x18 or 0x19.
    Confirm scans and device identity/configuration; an address ACK alone is not
    proof that the intended sensor is present. [S1, S6, S8]

E2. Power the INA260 logic from 3.3 V so its SDA/SCL pullups match the MCU.
    For an eventual high-side measurement:
      battery positive -> INA260 Vin+ -> internal shunt -> Vin- -> drone positive;
      battery negative -> drone negative, with a reviewed common logic reference.
    Vcc powers the sensor electronics; it is not the motor-power input. Vin+ and
    Vin- are opposite ends of the series shunt, NOT battery positive and negative.
    Identify the VBus sensing node: the Adafruit board normally ties it to Vin+.
    Name the logged voltage accordingly, rather than claiming motor-terminal
    voltage after wiring losses. [S6]
    Keep motor current out of breadboards, QT cables and logic jumper wires.
    Select power leads/connectors using the actual current, connector and battery
    evidence. The INA260 chip's 15 A headline does not rate the full harness. [S7]

E3. Connect bridge excitation to E+/E- and differential signal to A+/A- using
    verified cell wiring, not color alone. Verify load sign with a known load.
    Budget bridge current I_exc = V_exc / R_bridge. At a 3.3 V ADC supply, choose
    a supported excitation setting with LDO headroom; the NAU7802 data sheet
    requires supply at least 0.3 V above its regulated analog output. A 2.4 V
    starting setting is a proposal to check against the delivered bridge and
    required sensitivity. Record excitation, gain, rate and calibration. [S2]
    ADC internal calibration is not calibration of force in newtons.

E4. Use the beam receiver's open-collector output with a pullup to MCU 3.3 V.
    Confirm clear/blocked polarity empirically. If the optics are supplied from
    5 V, the signal pullup still belongs at 3.3 V for this MCU. [S9]
    Reserve an unused digital pin after the complete pin map is reviewed.
    Beam sensing marks a crossing; it does not command a motor cut or mechanically
    define release unless a separate verified arrangement establishes that link.

E5. Inventory a USB data cable, header soldering requirements, insulation, mounting
    hardware and suitable power connectors. One QT cable does not interconnect
    all listed boards. Use existing headers/jumpers where suitable; identify exact
    missing connections before proposing purchases. Secure connections before
    vibrating tests; loose bench jumpers are not an airborne harness.

E6. Leave drone power untouched until connector polarity, accessible rail,
    current budget and grounding scheme are known. Do not carry over the previous
    7.0 V propulsion test setting or assume a 5 V accessory output on this drone.
    Use the accepted V995 manufacturer specification: 3.7 V/180 mAh battery and
    5 V DC/2 A charger-adapter input. Retire the earlier battery-label verification
    task. Board rail identification remains necessary for an accessory connection.
    A future battery-powered QT Py needs a specific regulator/protection design,
    mass and voltage-range checks. USB operation does not establish that design.

8. R3 — CALIBRATE INSTALLED FORCE, NOT JUST ADC COUNTS
===================================================

R3.1 Draw the free-body diagram for the selected load direction. Define fixed end,
     loaded end, force application point, preload, clearance and load sign. Show
     every restraint/cable force. The intended measurement component must pass
     through the cell; another rigid support must not bypass it. The sensing
     section must remain free to deform. A load cell operating in bending can
     still implement the repo's direct-force route; do not confuse its internal
     mechanism with an external force-multiplying lever.

R3.2 Resolve the installed load budget before selecting 1 kg versus 5 kg. Include
     article/adapter weight, expected force direction, maximum credible applied
     force and disturbance loads. Check mount and bench attachment separately.
     An unknown thrust maximum must be bounded before a powered test, not used
     as a reason to assume the maximum is small. Keep spinning-prop work conditional
     on the actual fixture review, remote stop and facility arrangements.

R3.3 Verify cell geometry, threads, usable engagement and load direction from the
     delivered part/drawing. Do not reuse Phidgets dimensions, motor-hole patterns
     or prop-hub tolerances. A whole-drone cradle uses different interfaces from
     the earlier single EX1103 motor fixture; document the scope explicitly.

R3.4 Establish a reference-mass record and installed calibration procedure.
     Record reference values/uncertainty, fixture, instrument IDs, conditions,
     excitation, gain, rate, raw sign and stabilization rule. If reference masses
     are not characterized, label the result a provisional scale check; it does
     not satisfy traceable force evidence. A kitchen scale's display resolution
     alone is not its uncertainty.

R3.5 Proposed sequence after the fixture/load range is established:
       warm up and record at least 60 seconds unloaded;
       zero plus five nonzero loads spanning the intended measurement range;
       three ascending/descending cycles, repositioning each load independently;
       at least 10 seconds of settled data per point;
       two held-out loads within range, excluded from fitting;
       a 60-second zero check at the end.
     The range refers to the intended experiment, not automatically full cell
     capacity. Record settling duration and save pre-settling data. Include the
     actual load direction; do not extrapolate a compression-only check into
     tension or reverse loading without evidence.

R3.6 Fit and inspect:
       F_ref [N] = m_ref [kg] * g [m/s^2]
       F_est [N] = k [N/count] * counts + b [N]
     Use standard gravity 9.80665 m/s^2 only with that convention recorded and
     its relevance to the intended accuracy assessed. Check residuals, independent
     holdouts, hysteresis, zero drift, repeated mounting and off-axis sensitivity.
     Preserve separate calibration IDs for each cell/configuration/mounting.
     Changing gain, excitation, mounting or load path requires a new validity check.

R3.7 Define useful resolution BEFORE evaluating results. Proposed exploratory
     target: resolve a 5% difference relative to a documented reference force
     F_ref_use. Require the expanded uncertainty of the intended force difference
     to be no more than half that effect, 0.025 * F_ref_use. This is a proposed
     engineering discrimination target, not an existing repo release threshold.
     Until F_ref_use and the uncertainty budget are justified, report absolute
     uncertainty/noise and leave the target undecided. Include reference, scale,
     repeatability, drift and mounting contributions; do not use sample SD alone.
     Account for correlation when two readings share calibration errors. More
     samples reduce some random error, not unknown systematic bias.
     As a planning example only, using the estimated 50 g hover-equivalent force
     of 0.49 N gives a 5% effect of about 0.0245 N and a proposed expanded
     difference-uncertainty target of about 0.0123 N (1.25 gram-force). Replace
     the reference with the measured configuration and declared experimental
     question before judging a calibration against this target.

R3.8 Publish the calibration plot, residuals, usable range and exclusions. Report
     PASS_FOR_STATED_STATIC_USE / PROVISIONAL / INADEQUATE with the named basis.
     Static calibration does not validate high-frequency thrust response or impact
     peak measurement. Do not use the 5 kg cell as a crash sensor by default.

Acceptance: either a documented installed calibration adequate for its stated
range and resolution, or a specific quantified shortfall and next corrective step.


9. R4 — VALIDATE MOTION, ELECTRICAL AND EVENT MEASUREMENTS
=======================================================

R4.1 LIS3DH: collect six stationary orientations (+/- each axis), about 10 seconds
     each. Check axis map, sign, offsets and scale against gravity, with mounting
     alignment uncertainty noted. Record the sensor-to-body orientation. Retain
     raw DC acceleration; a high-pass-only stream cannot support gravity checks.
     Flag saturation per axis and all range/mode changes. [S8, S11]

R4.2 INA260: compare zero and at least two known dummy-load operating points to
     suitable voltage/current references, with their uncertainty recorded. Check
     current direction, units, conversion configuration and sensing-node identity.
     Prefer a characterized resistive load/reference arrangement over putting an
     unfamiliar handheld current input directly into the drone circuit.
     Compare electrical power P = V * I using time-aligned readings. Energy is
     the time integral of power; use actual sample intervals and explicit gaps.

R4.3 Beam: log at least 30 manually controlled block/clear cycles and compare the
     observed transitions to a separate count. Acceptance for this exercise: no
     unexplained missing/extra transition. That does not establish a failure rate
     or submillisecond accuracy. Check actual spacing and ambient-light conditions.
     The listed response time is under 2 ms; software timestamps add uncertainty.
     Use this device for coarse event timing, not assumed propeller RPM. [S9]

R4.4 Define t_event, t_ready/t_read and host receipt time separately. All MCU
     records share one monotonic clock, but ADC/filter latency still differs.
     Bound software timestamp uncertainty and document unresolved sensor delay.
     For a force/beam comparison, use a known load engaging the fixture while an
     attached flag crosses a measured beam location; retain the physical timing
     offset rather than treating both signals as an instantaneous common event.

R4.5 Analyze observability before promising new outputs:
     - LIS3DH gives specific force, not angular rate. Gravity direction can aid
       static tilt; arbitrary acceleration and free fall prevent that shortcut.
     - With a body-to-world rotation R, at the CG:
         f_body = transpose(R) * (a_world - g_world).
       An offset sensor also feels angular-acceleration and centripetal terms.
       Record its position; without angular data, do not claim to remove them.
     - One axial cell measures one force component. Aggregate drone thrust is
       not four individual thrusts and does not establish roll/pitch/yaw torque.
     - One current sensor in the battery lead measures aggregate draw.
     - One beam supplies one crossing event. Speed from blockage duration needs
       known geometry/orientation; a full trajectory needs additional observations.
     - No purchased sensor in this list establishes calibrated motor RPM or
       motor temperature. Missing channels in the old evidence contract stay missing.

R4.6 Deliver an aligned plot with force, voltage/current/power, acceleration and
     event markers only for quantities present in that run. Expose missing data,
     uncertainty, clipping and filter settings. Interpolation is a derived view;
     it must not create apparently measured samples in the raw record.

Acceptance: a replayable multi-sensor record with validated signs/units, an explicit
clock/latency model and a table stating what each measurement can support.
