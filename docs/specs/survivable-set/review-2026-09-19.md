## 3. Plan critique and revisions

The handoff plan is right on every finding and right on order. Revisions applied:

1. **Pull trivial confirmed defects forward.** F03 and F09 are one-function fixes with
   a test; leaving them for W02/W06 would mean running Day-2 diagnostics on a runner
   that can overwrite results and a verdict function that kills on empty input.
   Done in this PR. W02 and W06 keep their remaining scope.
2. **Cut the manifest.** The proposed 15-field manifest, trial JSONL and status
   enums are more infrastructure than a single-owner repo needs this week. What the
   acceptance criteria actually require: (a) quick/smoke cannot touch tracked
   results — done; (b) a trial is replayable — every trial is already a pure function
   of `(base_seed, variant_idx, tier, class_idx, cell_idx, action_idx, chunk_idx,
   trial_idx)`; W02 exposes that tuple as `trial_id` in the row and adds one replay
   test, nothing more; (c) truncated ≠ complete — done for the verdict, W02 adds the
   grid-coverage check. Source SHA + command + Python/NumPy go in the evidence README,
   not a schema.
3. **Merge the seven proposed documents into four.** This review (amended as the week
   proceeds), the scenario contract (W03), the paired-diagnostic protocol (W05), the
   decision memo (W08). Interpretation amendments are dated sections *in this file*;
   the Study C protocol draft replaces — not accompanies — the current prediction
   file's operating sections.
4. **W07 uses the registers that exist.** Accepted numbers go only in
   `cad/bench/parameters.csv`; requests are already in `input-requests.csv`. Day 1
   adds the intake packet and two raw-observation CSV templates in `evidence/`, not
   a third register or new worksheets inside `fixture-definition.md`.
5. **Compute is not the constraint.** The W05 pilot (576 runs ≈ 1 s each) is ~2 min on
   8 workers; benchmark once, then run the full predeclared design rather than a
   subset. Keep two workers only as the default on an unknown machine.
6. **Environment drift is itself a finding.** #22's CI ran on 3.11; the #22 local
   evidence was 3.13.7 / NumPy 2.5.3; today's baseline is 3.11.8 / 2.1.1. Results
   matched (129 OK) but W02's replay test must state the pinned environment.

## 4. Not done on Day 1, deliberately

No new sweep, no controller change, no allocator change, no threshold change, no
merge. The unchanged EST-REC-007 bench requirement (0.020 N·m at 7.0 V, 25–75 %
collective, `measured_authority_gate.py`) answers *does the vehicle have the
authority the sim assumes*; this study answers *given that authority, which action*.
They are separate gates and neither substitutes for the other.
